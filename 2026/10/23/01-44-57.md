On 2026-10-13 10:48, Brian Inglis wrote:
> The latest word about the ICU 79/CLDR 49 support of Western Canadian time zones 
> changes is that they will be maintaining the DST fiction year round from 
> release, starting 2026-11-01: using PDT, MDT, CDT for BC, AB, MB, year round, 
> rather than TZ DB's use of normal STD times using MST, CST, EST year round; 
> presumably YT stays on MST and SK on CST even under CLDR, so they will appear to 
> be on different time zones, and NT(/NWT) will follow AB, although NU regions 
> still appear undecided but likely to follow other adjacent regions.
>
> Do some tweaks need made to accommodate the changes coming in a few weeks, just 
> before the TZ and TZ DB change already updated?


I downloaded the CLDR 49 file with windowsZones.xml and an issue explained in 
CLDR-19872.txt based on win32tzlist.out which has not been applied to the 
released CLDR, updated windowsZones-cldr-49.xml to get 
windowsZones-cldr-19872.xml, and ran an adapted tzmap-windowsZones script to 
produce respectively tzmap-cldr-49.h and tzmap-cldr-19872.h and produced the 
included diffs showing the minor different output and fairly different inputs.

It would be good if someone other than myself could review these sources, 
changes, and products, before thay are built into Cygwin tzset by Nov 1, so 
Cygwin works properly in Western Canada and other recently changed Windows time 
zones.
