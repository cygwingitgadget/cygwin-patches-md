Hi Everyone,

This is the x86_64 counterpart of "Cygwin: gendef: restore incyg in
AArch64 longjmp", as discussed with Jon in that thread.

x86_64 longjmp also clears _cygtls.incyg instead of restoring the value
setjmp saw.  When setjmp runs inside Cygwin (a pthread_once initializer,
a DLL constructor) and a signal handler siglongjmps back, incyg wraps to
0xffffffff and signals are no longer delivered while the thread is
blocked.  It does not show up with OpenSSL on x86_64, which uses cpuid
rather than a SIGILL probe, but the reproducer from the AArch64 patch
(winsup.api/pthread/once3) fails there too.

jmp_buf has no free 8-byte slot on x86_64, so the patch keeps incyg in
the unused 16-bit Spare field of the Windows _JUMP_BUFFER layout (offset
0x5e).  The jmp_buf size and layout do not change.

Testing, built on the 3.6.12 sources for x86_64:

  - reproducer: FAIL 5/5 without the patch (SIGALRM not delivered, poll
    sleeps the full 5 s), PASS 5/5 with it (EINTR after 1000 ms).

Thanks,
Aswin
